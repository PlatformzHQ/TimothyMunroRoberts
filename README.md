# Platformz

## AI-Powered Distribution Infrastructure as a Service (DIaaS)

Platformz is an enterprise AI platform that helps manufacturers, distributors, wholesalers, retailers, and commerce brands automate and orchestrate their entire business from a single intelligent operating platform.

Built around modern enterprise architecture, Platformz connects ERP, PIM, DAM, OMS, WMS, CRM, 3PLs, marketplaces, retailers, shipping providers, finance systems, and AI agents into one unified ecosystem.

Our mission is simple:

> **Build intelligent software that eliminates manual work, connects every business system, and enables organizations to scale globally through automation and Artificial Intelligence.**

---

# What Platformz Does

Platformz provides an enterprise operating layer that enables organizations to:

- Build enterprise commerce ecosystems
- Connect hundreds of business applications
- Automate workflows using AI
- Synchronize inventory across channels
- Manage global product information
- Orchestrate fulfillment worldwide
- Monitor business operations in real time
- Predict operational issues before they happen
- Scale internationally with reusable integrations
